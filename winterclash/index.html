Questions

Who can enter?

Juniors in the U13, U15, U17 and U19 age groups with under 1,500 Badminton Ontario ranking points.

What do players need to bring to check-in?

Photo ID showing date of birth. Players without ID at check-in are disqualified, so please don't forget it. Bring your racket, court shoes and water.

When do we get our match times?

Groups are published Dec 16, and every player gets a personalized match schedule by email before the tournament.

How many matches will my child play?

Every event starts with a group stage in groups of 3, so every player gets multiple matches before the playoffs.

How do I enter doubles?

Both partners register and pay separately for the same doubles event, and each enters the other's name. If you're also in singles, use code CLASH2 on your doubles entry.

Is there a deadline for shirt sizes?

Yes. Register by Friday, Dec 4 to guarantee your size. Every player gets a tournament shirt, and we order them 15 days before the tournament, so for entries after Dec 4 we can't guarantee the size you pick.

What if a draw is full?

Each singles draw is capped at 12 players and each doubles draw at 6 pairs. After that, new entries join the waitlist.

What is the refund policy?

If TBP cancels your event and can't combine it with another, you get a full refund. Otherwise entries are non-refundable.

Can we leave between matches?

Yes. Between the group stage and playoffs you can take a break and come back. Check your match times so you're back on time.
