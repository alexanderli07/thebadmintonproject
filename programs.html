The Academy

TheAcademy.

One pathway for juniors, and adult training alongside it — every session a deliberate step in a longer plan.

The Programs

Five programs, one standard.

Four junior programs run as one pathway — a player joins where they are and moves up as they earn it — with adult training alongside. Each is planned with the same care, whether your child is chasing titles or finding their first competitive rally. Nothing here is improvised.

  1. 01

    Beginner Training

    Where it starts. Players learn the game the right way from day one — the core strokes and the mechanics behind them: the clear, the drive, the lunge, and the footwork that ties it all together, built from a stable base so every stroke is learned correctly before we add speed.

    Explore the program
  2. 02

    Intermediate Training

    The next step. The same strokes go into motion — complexity added and every shot sharpened, shifting from a static base to shuffling, recovering and hitting from real match positions. This is where fundamentals become a real playing game.

    Explore the program
  3. 03

    High Performance Team Training

    The tournament pathway. Our High Performance players qualify and compete at provincial and national tournaments, with our coaches travelling alongside them to compete. It’s where developing players become real competitors.

    Explore the program
  4. 04

    National Team Elite Training

    Our highest tier — training alongside coaches who compete at the national and international level. Built for provincial and national-level players only, training to the same standard we hold ourselves to on the world circuit. Limited spots, by selection.

    Explore the program

Adult Training

Off the junior pathway — the same coaching standard for grown players, one-on-one, with a partner, or in a small group at your level.

See adult training

Beyond the Junior Academy

Adults train seriously here too.

Whether you’re picking the sport back up, training to sharpen your game, or getting serious about competing. Some come to improve for the love of it and to hold their own against friends. Others train to compete at tournaments and push themselves at a higher level. Wherever you’re starting, you’ll get real coaching built around your goals.

  • Private Training — 1:1

    One-on-one sessions built entirely around you. In-depth focus, a personalized plan, fast and measurable improvement.

  • Semi-Private Training — 1:2

    The same personalized coaching, shared with one other player — perfect for training with your partner or a friend.

  • Group Lessons

    Focused sessions with players at your level. The most cost-efficient way to train seriously without losing personal attention.
