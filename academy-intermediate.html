The Program

What to expect.

The next step. Players take the same strokes — clear, drive, lunge, and movement — and put them into motion. We add complexity and sharpen every shot, shifting from a static base to active, on-the-move execution: shuffling, recovering, and hitting from real match positions. This is where solid fundamentals start to become a real playing game.

Who it’s for
Players who already have the core strokes and are ready to take them off a static base and into motion.
What they’ll train
The same strokes — clear, drive, lunge and movement — with complexity added and every shot sharpened: shuffling, recovering, and hitting from real match positions.
What they walk away with
A real playing game — fundamentals that hold up on the move, and the sharpness to use them in a live rally.
