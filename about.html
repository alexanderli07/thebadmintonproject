Who We Are

The people behindthe Project.

The Badminton Project exists to give kids and teens genuine coaching and competitive development — the kind that’s planned, purposeful, and taken seriously every session.

Who We Are

Every young athlete begins as a project.

We develop young athletes deliberately: beginners learning the game the right way from day one, developing players finding their competitive edge, and committed juniors chasing the top of the sport.

Whatever the level, every child gets the same thing — a real coach, a real plan, and real standards.

Progress here is earned step by step. That’s not a limitation. That’s the promise — and it’s why our athletes actually improve.

What We Stand For

Three things we hold to.

  • Excellence

    Real standards in every session, for every age and level. A beginner’s class is coached as seriously as a competitive squad. The bar doesn’t move.

  • Development

    Young athletes are built, not found. Every session is one deliberate step in a longer plan — and we can show you where your child is on it.

  • Community

    Kids train harder when they belong somewhere. We build a group your child wants to show up for, alongside coaches who know their name and their game.
