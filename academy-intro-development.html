The Program

What to expect.

Where it starts. Players learn the game the right way from day one — the core strokes and the mechanics behind them: the clear, the drive, the lunge, and the footwork that ties it all together. At this stage we build clean technique from a stable, static base, so every stroke is learned correctly before we add speed. Get the foundation right, and everything after it comes easier.

Who it’s for
New and developing young players — kids and teens learning the game properly from the start, so nothing has to be un-learned later.
What they’ll train
Sound technique from the ground up — grip, footwork, the core strokes, and early match craft. We build correct habits early, because the difference between a coached beginner and a supervised one shows up years down the line.
What they walk away with
A real foundation — clean technique, confident movement, and genuine readiness to step into competitive training when they’re ready.
